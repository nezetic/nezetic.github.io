  • Spring is here!

    For many reasons (including the heaviness and the numerous personal hacks that made updates a nightmare), I decided to change the site engine.

    Goodbye Drupal then, and welcome Textpattern.

    For those who do not know it, it is a CMS like dozens of others, but it brings me: relative lightness, a monstrous template system, very intuitive editing (thanks in particular to the integration of textile), a sober and extremely well-designed administration console, plus dozens of little things that make you quickly happy to have changed shop.

    By the way, and as the title of this news says, spring has already been here for a few days, the good weather is back, and the site takes the opportunity to get a new look.

    Not that the old design was used much, but after nearly a year of existence, I wanted to show other colours.
