  • My precious...

    In order to escape the nasty rays of the sun that-burns-the-skin-and-explodes-the-eyes on this fine July Sunday (and because at 32°C in the shade, rollerblading turns into a masochism session), I decided to put a bit of free time to good use and make an addition to this little site that is dear to me.

    It is a kind of gallery presenting the Unix machines that I keep and take care of. Far from being a simple list of names of no interest, each one has its own identity sheet, with fairly complete information, as well as pretty images for people who do not like to read (and who therefore will not read this, no risk of getting on their bad side).

    Of course, this section will be constantly evolving, both in terms of the machines and the information about them.

    That’s it. I know that personally I very much enjoy browsing the collections of certain enthusiasts, and to be honest, it will also let me take stock of what I own.

    For the rest, it all happens in the collection section.
