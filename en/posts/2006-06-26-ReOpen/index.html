  • Official (Re)Opening

    Hello Web,

    Well, after a year without news, I have decided to reopen my personal page.

    The reason is simple: a need for expression, the desire to share my experiences and my knowledge, mainly related to computing, and to UNIX systems in particular.

    A desire also to distribute the fruit of my work, notably free software projects on which I spend part of my free time.

    A new start, therefore, which involves quite a few new things: a change of host, a new site with an updated design, more flexibility in content management thanks to the use of the Drupal CMS.

    Of course, quite a few things are still to be put in place; the site is for now reduced to its minimum, but the content will come little by little, as updates go.

    With that, there is nothing left but to break the champagne bottle on the hull of my new vessel, and wish it a journey full of good things on the ocean that the Internet is today.

    by Cédric TESSIER on 2006-06-26
